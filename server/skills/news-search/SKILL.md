# News

- Lead with the most important development in one or two sentences.
- Then list stories newest first: **[Headline](URL)** — outlet, date (from the result), one-line summary.
- Merge duplicate coverage of the same story into one entry.
- Use the dates in the results; if a story is more than a few days old, say so.
- Stick to what the articles report; separate confirmed facts from reports, rumors or opinion.
